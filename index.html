Pocketlog Privacy Policy

Developer: the_mad_hatter
Last updated: 28 October 2026

Pocketlog is an offline expense tracker. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your financial records. No account is required.

Data and security

Income and expense records, categories, monthly plans, preferences and backup settings are processed on your device to provide tracking, summaries and backups. The ledger is encrypted; encryption keys are protected by Android Keystore.

Optional app lock uses Android authentication. Pocketlog does not receive your fingerprint data or phone PIN, pattern or password. This version has no Internet permission, advertising, analytics or telemetry.

Backups and exports

You can export password-encrypted backups or schedule them in a local folder you choose. The automatic-backup password is stored encrypted on your device. Lost backup passwords cannot be recovered.

CSV reports are readable and unencrypted. Pocketlog does not automatically upload files. If you choose a cloud document provider or share an exported file, that service handles it under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them, restore another archive, clear app data or uninstall. Restoring replaces the active archive. Exported files remain separate and must be deleted from their saved locations.

Automatic backups normally retain seven recent copies for the current configuration; older configurations and passwords are preserved, and failed cleanup may leave extra copies. Removing backup settings stops scheduled backups and removes the saved password without deleting existing files. The developer cannot access or recover your local records.

Support and this website

For privacy questions, email themadhatterapp@libero.it. Messages you send are handled by the email providers involved and used and retained as needed to answer your request or meet legal obligations. Do not send passwords or financial records.

This page has no analytics, cookies or tracking scripts. Its hosting provider may process technical requests, including IP addresses and browser details, to serve the page.

Audience and changes

Pocketlog is intended for users aged 13 and older. This policy will be updated if its data handling changes.
